Local selectivity (σl): how often the filter fires among the query’s k nearest neighbors.
Global selectivity (σg): how often the filter fires in the whole set.
Selectivity ratio (r): r > 1 enrichment, r < 1 depletion.
GLS correlation (ρ): Mapping GLS ratio to a range [−1, 1) using bilinear (Möbius) transformation. Positive → easier filtered queries; negative → harder.
